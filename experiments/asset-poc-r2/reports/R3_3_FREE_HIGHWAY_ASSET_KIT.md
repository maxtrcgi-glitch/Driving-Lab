## Verification boundaries

* New downloads attempted from this environment: `https://ambientcg.com/get?file=Asphalt001_1K-JPG.zip` returned HTTP 302 to `acg-download.struffelproductions.com`; the redirected connection timed out after 12 seconds. No archive was obtained. This result is specific to that URL and does not establish that the candidate providers are inaccessible to the Director.
* New shortlisted assets were **not** independently downloaded or rendered; Fab sign-in/download and licensing flow, Poly Haven downloads, and Innerscene Jersey download have not been validated in this session. Actual formats, texture dimensions, alpha maps, mesh bounds and SHA-256 are UNKNOWN until files are obtained. Existing R2.2a and R3.1 verification is explicitly prior work, not a new test.
* Intended Three.js interpretation after verification: base-color textures in sRGB; normal, roughness and opacity treated as non-color data; use OpenGL normal maps or confirm handedness. glTF can be inspected with GLTFLoader; Fab glTF/decal import and material compatibility are not yet tested.
* Fab Standard commonly permits using assets within a project but forbids distributing them standalone. Confirm entitlement and the intended publicly accessible WebGL asset delivery; do not commit or publish raw Fab files based on this report alone.
* Preview galleries are source marketing images, not proof of visual quality in the game. The existing road tests revealed obvious repetition; do not treat previous procedural prototypes as approved assets.
* Specific open gaps: actual free source files and visual validation for road wear, white marking, shoulder and concrete barrier; a separately verified realistic drainage channel and European reflector posts were not selected. No source-level claim about a seamless asphalt-to-gravel transition. Director approval is required before integration.

No new model/texture was generated and no highway was assembled. Keep the original accepted-for-preparation guardrail and all prior checkpoints intact.
