# R4.1c vegetation and reference

- [A9 north of Munich](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Auf_der_A_9_in_Richtung_Berlin.jpg), Kira Nerys, public domain. Art cues are the low grass at the rail, dense irregular undergrowth uphill, and forest layers beyond the immediate verge.
- [Grass Medium 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/grass_medium_02) and [Grass Bermuda 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/grass_bermuda_01), Poly Haven [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license). The workflow fetches the original provider glTF mesh packages with 1K textures. The artifact records every URL and SHA-256 in `vegetation-download-manifest.json`. Powered by Poly Haven.
- [Tree Small 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/tree_small_02) and [Pine Sapling Medium](https://polyhaven.com/a/pine_sapling_medium), Poly Haven CC0. Reused as packed source meshes from R4.1b, with the established individual tree anchors. The broadleaf source is also used at sapling and low scrub scale.
- Existing Leafy Grass and Gravel Road ground maps, road and barrier assets, and HDRI remain as documented in the [R4.1 source asset manifest](../ASSET_MANIFEST.md).
