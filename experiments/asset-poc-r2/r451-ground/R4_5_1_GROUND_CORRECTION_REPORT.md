# R4.5.1 ground correction

Status — NOT READY for the artistic quality gate. A recoverable ground-only correction is delivered for inspection.

## Normal correction

R4.5 blended encoded normal RGB through MixRGB. Both the historical author_materials.py ground path and the new R4.5.1 graph use decoded vectors. For each Non-Color OpenGL normal sample c, n = normalize(2c − 1). For alternative scan samples or ground-cover regions, the result is normalize((1 − w)n₀ + wn₁). It is then encoded as 0.5n + 0.5 and passed once through Blender's tangent Normal Map node. These are alternative surface regions, so normalized interpolation is used rather than layering one relief field over another with RNM.

All scan samples share the original Surface metres UV layer. The secondary sample uses the same translation (0.371, 0.619) for diffuse, roughness and normal. There is no rotation, reflection or scale change, so both decoded normals already share one tangent frame. The tangent-to-world conversion happens once after normalized compositing. Normal and roughness images are Non-Color data.

The older R4.5 authoring script remains reproducible with its ground normal blending corrected. The R4.5.1 native project is generated by correct_ground.py from the saved R4.5 working project.

## Roadside treatment

Only three ground materials are replaced. Original UVs, meshes and packed images are retained. The original Poly Haven Gravel Road and Leafy Grass inputs remain the scan sources, with their CC0 provenance inherited from the R4.1/R4.5 manifests.

The material calculates lateral distance from the existing 630 m route radius and the existing edges at +13.8 m and −17 m. A cover transition spans approximately 0.35–1.8 m beyond the edge, with up to ±0.45 m variation from a bounded world-space selection field. This provides a mineral strip near traffic and more low cover farther away. It is a material coverage mask, not new vegetation geometry or soil displacement.

The gravel and grass color treatments are kept separate before coverage mixing. A second translated sample is selected with a shared 10 m field and a 0.46–0.54 transition interval across all PBR channels. This preserves source detail in the selected regions. Blend boundaries can still lose detail. No measured anti-tiling improvement or physically measured compaction is claimed.

## Visual evidence and limits

Before and after use Blender 4.3.2 Cycles, 1152×648, 64 maximum samples, adaptive threshold 0.035 and denoising disabled. The installed Blender lacks OpenImageDenoise. Driver Eye and Roadside retain the original camera transforms, lighting and exposure. The additional close-up uses an identical temporary inspection camera for both versions. It is never saved in the native project.

The new mineral strip is visibly different from the R4.5 green verge. The close-up still exposes an overly regular brown band. Changes in color and normal response do not resolve the clean geometric asphalt/ground edge. The existing gravel scan reads as fine brown compacted soil at this framing and does not provide a coarse, irregular crumbling road edge. The existing flat edge profile has no loose aggregate silhouette or irregular erosion geometry. Those limitations remain visible instead of being obscured by more procedural variation.

The complete natural-transition criterion is not accepted. The remaining issue is visual quality, not shader compilation. Further procedural tuning is stopped at this checkpoint. A future decision would need either a better edge-specific scan/mask with discrete mineral/dust structure, or explicitly authorized edge geometry/decal work. No free replacement has been selected or downloaded in this correction.

## Reproduction and recovery

Start from R4_5_Roadside_Material_Library.blend in the saved R4.5 source archive. Run Blender with correct_ground.py and an output .blend path after --. render_ground.py takes an output directory, with --close for the temporary inspection view. Run both source and candidate through the same render script.

The source archive contains the packed R4.5.1 working project, editing and render scripts, report and checksums. The evidence archive contains the six full-resolution PNGs and three same-camera comparisons. The report's GitHub revision and archive hashes are recorded in verification.json and the final delivery message.
